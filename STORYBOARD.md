# ORANGE CLOUD COFFEE

## Creative concept

**The morning, with a twist.** A familiar café ritual turns into a bright surprise: freshly cut orange, the sound of ice, a slow espresso pour, and the cloud of cream. The final drink feels refreshing and indulgent at once.

**Audience:** Coffee curious adults, café regulars, and short-form food viewers aged roughly 20–40 who value a distinctive, photogenic afternoon or morning drink.

**Visual direction:** Realistic product photography; warm morning sun; travertine countertop; dark café background; golden orange, deep espresso, ivory foam. Macro detail, condensation, shallow depth of field, and small camera pushes give the stills a cinematic rhythm. The drink uses one straight-sided low tumbler with a thick clear base across the sequence.

**Tagline:** Coffee. Citrus. Unexpected.

## Five-shot storyboard

| Shot | Time | Picture and action | Motion and sound |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 — Fresh | 0:00–0:03.21 | Orange is sliced in foreground; finished glass sits softly out of focus behind it. | Slow push in; subtle citrus cut and quiet café room tone. |

| 4 — Cloud | 0:08.88–0:12.08 | The orange, espresso, and cream layers settle into a cloudlike pattern; an orange wheel rests on the rim. | Slow push in; soft airy texture in the sound design. |
| 5 — Hero | 0:11.83–0:15.00 | Condensation and the full drink, centered. Exact product name and tagline appear in the upper portion. | Small pull back; room tone and a restrained synthetic tonal accent. |

Shots overlap by 0.25 seconds with dissolves. The final title appears at approximately 12.15 seconds. Short, quiet chapter words appear above shots 1–4.
